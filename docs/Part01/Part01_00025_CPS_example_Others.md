# Other Examples of Cyber-Physical Systems

Cyber-physical systems extend beyond the robots, pacemakers, and vehicles discussed so far. They also appear in everyday household devices, healthcare technologies, energy infrastructure, and satellite systems. Although these applications differ in scale and purpose, they share a common principle: computation is closely integrated with physical processes through sensing, communication, and actuation.

- [Cleaning robots](https://aitimejournal.com/can-robot-vacuums-clean-multiple-rooms/)
- [Smart Lighting](https://tvilight.com/smart-city-begins-with-smart-street-lighting/)
- [Smart heating system/smart home](https://ehi.eu/heating-technologies/smart-heating-home/) and  [@Chen2017]
- [Insulin pumps](https://www.xcode.life/genes-and-ai/ai-diabetes-care-prevention-treatment/
) and [@BHC+2021]
- Smart prosthetics/wearable technologies [@DAS+2018,KSK+2023]
- Energy sector including windmills and smart grids [@MMA+2026]
- [Starlink](wiki:Starlink)

:class: important
