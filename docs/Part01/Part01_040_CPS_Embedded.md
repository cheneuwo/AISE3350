# Embedded Systems

An **[Embedded Systems](wiki:Embedded_system)** is a computational system embedded within a larger physical system. Many cyber-physical systems contain one or more embedded systems.

The term *embedded system* reflects a primary focus on the computational component that is embedded within the larger physical system. Traditionally, research and development in embedded systems have focused
on topics such as:

- formal verification of discrete models, such as automata;
- hardware design;
- minimizing energy consumption and production costs; and
- embedded-software development.

The CPS perspective emphasizes the importance of considering the **physical context** in which the computational system operates. This context is often necessary when defining, designing, testing, and
verifying the required functionality.

An embedded-system perspective primarily asks:

> How can the computational component be implemented correctly, efficiently, and reliably?

A CPS perspective additionally asks:

> How does the computational component interact with, monitor, and control the physical process?

The terms *embedded system* and *cyber-physical system* are therefore closely related but are not synonymous. An embedded system is generally a component of a larger system, whereas CPS refers to the combined behaviour of the computation, communication, control, and physical process.
