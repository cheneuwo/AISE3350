# Vehicle to Everything (V2X)

[Vehicle-to-everything (V2X)](wiki:Vehicle-to-everything), is an umbrella terms that describes wireless communication between a vehicle and any thtity that *may affect*, or *may be affected by*, the vehicle.

Industry standards are evolving and perhaps not widely adopted at the time of this writing (Sept. 2026)

- [Dedicated short-range communication (DSRC)](wiki:Dedicated_short-range_communications) is a technology for **direct** wireless exchange of [V2X](wiki:Vehicle-to-everything) and other [intelligent transportation systems (ITS)](wiki:Intelligent_transportation_system) data between vehicles, other road users, and roadside infrastructures. 
  - DSRC uses channels in the licensed 6.9GHz band, and is based on [IEEE 802.11p](wiki:IEEE_802.11p).
- [Cellure V2X (C-V2X)](wiki:Cellular_V2X) is an attempt to standardize [V2X](wiki:Vehicle-to-everything) based on the [Long Term Evolution (LTE)](wiki:LTE_(telecommunication)) as the underlying communication technology. In addition to the direct communication (Vehicle-to-vehicle and [Vehicle-to-Infrastructure](wiki:Vehicle_infrastructure_integration)), it also support wide area communication over a cellular network (Vehicle-to-Network).

| Relationship | Participants | Example |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| V2V | Vehicle and nearby behicles | Position, speed, and braking information |
| V2P | Vehicle and equipped road users | Warning involving pedestrians and other volnerable road users (e.g. wheelchair) |
| V2N | Vehicle and a wider network | Telematics, fleet management |
| V2I | Vehicle and roadside infrastructure | Traffic signal information, road warning |
| V2D | Vehicle and devices | Bluetooth/Wifi-direct, e.g. Android Auto and Apply CarPlay |
| V2G | Vehicle and energy grid | Energy load sharing/balancing |
| V2C | Vehicle and cloud service | OTA update, remote diagnoses |
