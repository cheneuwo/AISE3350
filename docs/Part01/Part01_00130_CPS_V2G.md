# Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G)

[Vehicle-to-grid (V2G)](wiki:Vehicle-to-grid) enables compatible [plug-in electric vehicles](wiki:Plug-in_electric_vehicle) to exchange electrical energy bidirectionally with the [electrical grid](wiki:Electrical_grid). Through coordinated charging and discharging, vehicle batteries can help balance electricity supply and demand. Depending on the utility programme, vehicle owners may receive compensation for providing these services.

Vehicle batteries can support the grid by:

- **Storing energy** when electricity generation is abundant or demand is low.
- **Returning energy to the grid** during periods of high demand, reducing the amount of electricity that other sources must supply.

A related application, **vehicle-to-home (V2H)**, allows a compatible vehicle to supply electricity to a home, potentially providing backup power during an outage. Unlike V2G, V2H supplies the home rather than exporting electricity to the wider grid. Both applications require compatible vehicles and charging equipment.

From a CPS perspective, communication coordinates when and how much a vehicle charges or discharges, while the physical interaction involves the transfer of electrical energy.
