# Relationship to Other Disciplines

Cyber-physical systems are closely related to several established engineering disciplines:
- Mechatronics, which integrates mechanical, electronic, and   computational components;
- Control theory, which studies the use of feedback to influence   system behaviour;
- Robotics, which combines sensing, perception, computation,   planning, and actuation; and
- the Internet of Things (IoT), which connects distributed sensors, devices, and services through communication networks.

Concepts and techniques from these disciplines will be introduced throughout this course when they contribute to the modelling, analysis, control, or design of cyber-physical systems.
