# Are Cyber-Physical Systems New?

Although the term *cyber-physical system* is relatively recent, many of its underlying ideas are not new. CPS provides a holistic, system-level perspective that brings together several established concepts and engineering concerns:

- [Hybrid systems](wiki:Hybrid_system)
- [Embedded systems](wiki:Embedded_system)
- [Real-time systems](wiki:Real-time_computing)
- [Reliability](wiki:Reliability_engineering)
- [Dependability](wiki:Dependability)

The significance of CPS lies not in introducing each of these ideas individually, but in examining how they interact when computation, communication, and control are tightly integrated with physical processes.
