# Vehicle-to-Cloud (V2C)

Vehicle-to-cloud (V2C) communication refers to the exchange of data between a vehicle and cloud-based systems through wireless network connections. This enables vehicles to send and receive information such as traffic updates, weather conditions, navigation data, and vehicle diagnostics.

V2C uses cloud computing to store, process, and analyse large amounts of data, providing useful information to drivers, manufacturers, and service providers. Processing can occur in near real time, depending on network connectivity, communication delays, and the application’s requirements.

## Applications Enabled by V2C

- **Over-the-air (OTA) updates:** Software components of a connected vehicle can be updated remotely. For example, [Tesla vehicles](https://www.tesla.com/en_ca/support/software-updates) receive OTA software updates that introduce new features and enhance existing functionality.

- **Remote vehicle diagnostics:** Vehicles can transmit diagnostic information, such as fault codes and operating data, to cloud-based systems. Manufacturers and service providers can use this information to assess vehicle condition, identify potential faults, and support maintenance planning.

- **Traffic efficiency:** Traffic congestion is a significant challenge in urban areas, leading to wasted time, increased fuel consumption, and higher emissions. Cloud-based systems can analyse traffic data collected from connected vehicles and other sources to suggest alternative routes. When integrated with traffic management infrastructure, these systems can also support adjustments to traffic signal timings and dynamic toll pricing.
